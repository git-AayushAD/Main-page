The Event

Vortex is a curated series of competitions, labs, and talks blending chemical engineering fundamentals with real-world industry practice. Every track delivers hands-on challenges, live demonstrations, and direct technical mentorship.

  • Live problem-solving labs and guided walkthroughs.
  • Industry panels on process design, safety, and bioprocessing.
  • Career-aligned workshops with certificates and feedback.

ICT Mumbai

Established in 1933 as UDCT and granted Deemed-to-be University status in 2008, the Institute of Chemical Technology is recognized globally for excellence in chemical engineering, materials sciences, and allied disciplines.
